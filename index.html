Intelligent Disaster Mitigation

RescueAI is composed of three specialized, integrated modules designed to handle distinct segments of emergency tracking and containment.

Disaster Alerts

Instant alert broadcasting system. Automatically triggers localized cellular broadcasts, SMS, and email updates to notify residents of oncoming hazards.

Multi-channel broadcast integration (cellular, SMS, web)
Sub-second message dispatch times
Geo-fenced targeting down to coordinates
Learn More

AI Risk Prediction

Analyzes meteorology trends, satellite radar feeds, and regional sensor grids. Builds spatial hazard risk heatmaps to project spreads hours before impact.

LSTM networks for flood stream telemetry forecasting
Thermal imaging analysis for wildfire boundaries
Geological signal parsing for seismic prediction
Learn More

Emergency Response

Intelligent logistical response routing. Generates block-free paths for rescue squads and links supply warehouses directly with shelters needing support.

Dynamic routing mapping around road blockages
Predictive first responder dispatch matrix
Automated disaster shelter supply distribution
